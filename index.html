Six ways to want a car

Every vehicle at VANTORA is placed within a collection: a point of view on what the machine is for, and who it is for.

Collection

Supercars

Performance philosophy

We do not sell cars that are merely fast. We represent machines whose engineering has a point of view, whose provenance can be proven, and whose next owner will understand both.

Founded in 2011 by two former factory engineers, VANTORA began as a private sourcing office for a handful of collectors. Fifteen years later the principle has not changed: fewer cars, chosen with more care. Every vehicle we offer has passed our 172-point inspection, its history has been verified to the last invoice, and its specification documented in full.

We are brand-agnostic by design. A GT3 RS with delivery mileage, a Black Badge Ghost, a LaFerrari with Classiche papers — what unites them is not a badge but a standard. If a car does not meet it, we will tell you, and we will help you find one that does.

Private sourcing

The car you want is somewhere. We find it.

Most of the vehicles we place never appear on this website. They are found, negotiated and delivered quietly on behalf of a single client.

Private
Acquisition
Stage 01

Tell us what you want

A model, a specification, a colour combination that was only offered for one year — or simply a feeling. We start with a conversation at the showroom, at your home, or by phone.

Stage 02

Global search

Our network spans collectors, factory relationships and private sellers across 38 countries. We know which cars exist, who owns them, and which owners might be persuaded.

Stage 03

Private acquisition

We negotiate on your behalf, discreetly and with a single objective: the right car at the right number. Your name is never disclosed until you choose.

Stage 04

Inspection

Every acquisition is subject to our 172-point inspection, paint-depth mapping, and a full provenance audit. If we find something, you hear it from us first.

Stage 05

Delivery

Enclosed transport, air freight, or a handover at the showroom. Registration, insurance and storage arranged. You simply collect the keys.

Collector services

Ownership, looked after

Beyond the sale: the services that keep a collection in the condition it deserves.

01

Vehicle acquisition & sale

Discreet brokerage of significant vehicles, including sale-by-consignment from our showroom with full marketing, photography and buyer vetting.

02

Collection management

Climate-controlled storage, exercise schedules, battery conditioning, MOT and service management, and a quarterly condition report on each vehicle.

03

Inspection & valuation

Pre-purchase inspections anywhere in Europe, independent valuations for insurance, and provenance research for auction or private sale.

04

Concierge delivery

Enclosed transport and air freight worldwide, with customs, registration and insurance handled by our team. Track-day and tour logistics on request.
